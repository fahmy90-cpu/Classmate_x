About This App

A free social app for classmates — direct messages, Global Hub, Stories with music, polls, voice notes, voice/video calls, scheduled messages, and more. No ads, no fees, no payments.

Key Features: DM • Stories • Polls • Voice & Video Calls • Music Cards • HD Wallpapers • Ghost Mode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect. We collect only what is necessary to run the app: your email address, display name, username, profile photo, bio, and the messages/stories you choose to share. We do not collect phone numbers, locations (only when you explicitly share location in a chat), or contacts (only when you explicitly share a contact).

2. How We Use It. Your data is used solely to: authenticate you, display your profile and messages to other users, deliver push notifications (via OneSignal), and enable voice/video calls (peer-to-peer via WebRTC — call audio/video never passes through our servers).

3. What We Never Do. We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers. We never read your private direct messages — DMs are stored encrypted in transit and are readable only by the sender and recipient.

4. Storage & Cookies. We use browser local storage and IndexedDB to keep the app fast and to store media on your own device. OneSignal uses browser storage to deliver notifications. Media shared in chats is automatically removed from our servers after 24 hours (a copy remains on the sender's and recipient's devices).

5. Your Rights. You may edit or delete your profile at any time (Profile → Edit). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and data. To request complete data removal, contact fahmymazrui@gmail.com.

6. Children. ClassmateX is intended for students. Users under 13 should use the app with a guardian's knowledge. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Contact. Questions about privacy? Email fahmymazrui@gmail.com.
